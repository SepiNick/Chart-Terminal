# Review of My Journal (web.my-journal.app), the journal reference for Chart Terminal

Review date: 2026-10-17, using the user's free (Lite) account, read-only; nothing was saved or changed. For the sections locked in the free plan, only their names were visible, and our design for them is our own. Our implementation is clean-room: we took only the capabilities, not the code, text or look.

## Sections (main menu)
| Section | Content | Status in our project |
|---|---|---|
| Workspace (dashboard) | Period filter (all, daily, weekly, monthly, yearly, custom), journal filter, dashboard customization. Balance cards (with deposits and withdrawals), win rate, average R, profit factor and journal count. Equity curve with a five-trade MA, profit and loss distribution, performance analysis (symbol, weekday, session, timeframe, entry reasons, exit reasons). Yearly GitHub-style activity map with the Jalali calendar. Buy vs. sell, performance summary (best and worst trade, recovery factor, Sharpe, absolute and relative drawdown, commission, swap, consecutive wins and losses). Risk distribution (under 1%, 1 to 2%, 2 to 3%, over 3%), checklist adherence, active stop loss, trades without a plan, sessions (Tokyo, London, New York, Sydney), and top symbols | Will be implemented |
| Journals | Table (title, symbol, direction, volume, timeframe, date, time, commission, actual R, expected R, result in dollars and percent, media), filter, sort, grouping, export, trading report card, column customization, smart statement upload, and pagination | Will be implemented (MT statement upload: CSV or HTML) |
| Journal form | Two modes: "Log a trade" and "Log a no-trade day" (symbol, timeframe, date, and why you did not trade). Tabs: essential information (symbol, direction, timeframe, entry date, profit or loss, volume, quantity, risk-free or break-even). Optional information (entry and exit price, initial and final stop loss and take profit, actual and expected RR, exit date, commission, swap). Entry and exit reasons (editable list and notes), trade notes, checklist, photo and voice, tags, emotions (10 emotions, up to 4 before and 4 after), energy 1 to 5, confidence in the setup 1 to 5, entry motivation (per strategy, FOMO, revenge on the market, intuition, someone else's advice, news, technical signal, fundamental signal, and a custom question). Crypto (trade value, futures or spot, exchange, contract type, leverage, margin type, liquidation price, funding and network fee). Customization: user-defined custom fields with type and order | Will be implemented |
| Checklist | Title, items, and in advanced mode a priority weight for each item | Will be implemented |
| Discipline path | Active days, level (bronze and above), streak, rank, today's score, today's path (activity log, journal scores, optional backtest), and the traders' league. **Rules:** each journal is worth at most 80 points: 7 items worth 10 points each (initial stop loss, entry reason, exit reason, notes, one checklist tick, one photo, one emotion) and 10 bonus points for completion. A no-trade day is worth 80 points. Every 5 backtest trades earn 20 points. The daily cap is 100, and the day's score is the average of that day's journals. The logging deadline is the end of the day. Profit and loss do not affect the score. A rest day does not break the streak | Will be implemented (no multiplayer league, since there is no server) |
| Backtest | Backtest session with candle replay, virtual trades, and converting trades into journals. Includes backtest accounts, quota, win rate, PF, and virtual profit and loss | With our own Bar Replay and a separate backtest account |
| Analyses | Daily analysis, psychological and behavioral, time and efficiency, execution quality and efficiency, and professional analytics (locked in the free plan) | Our own design |
| Tools | Prop (name, phase, profit target %, overall drawdown %, daily drawdown %, drawdown type fixed or trailing, minimum trading days, and automatic monitoring from the journal), economic calendar, forex clock, market prices, resume builder, and account comparison | Prop, forex clock, resume and comparison will be implemented. The economic calendar is in phase 9. Market prices are the same as our Watchlist |
| Pro notes | Notes list and editor | Will be implemented |
| Accounts | Multiple accounts, each with a name, initial balance, and deposits and withdrawals | Will be implemented |
| Automatic Expert Advisor | An EA for MT4/MT5 that logs trades automatically | We log automatically from the practice account. Connecting to MT5 is in phase 11 |
| AI, collaboration, subscription | — | Dropped |
